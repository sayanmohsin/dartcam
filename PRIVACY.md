# Privacy Policy for DartCam

Last updated: July 2026

## Data Collection
DartCam does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No accounts are required to use the app.

## Camera
Photos taken with the camera are processed entirely on-device for dart detection. They are never uploaded, shared, or transmitted off the device.

## Cloud Sync (optional)
If you configure thingd.cloud sync, only aggregate match results (winner name, final scores, game type) are sent to the cloud. No turn-by-turn data, no individual dart scores, and no personal information are transmitted.

## Third-Party Services
DartCam uses no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting SDKs, and no third-party services that collect user data.

## Data Storage
All game data (match history, scores, player names) is stored locally on your device using the thingd embedded database engine.

## Changes to This Policy
If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will be updated.
